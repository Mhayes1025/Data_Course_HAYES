Interpretation

The exploratory analysis suggests that GPA is higher on average for admitted students compared to non-admitted students. Model Interpretation

The logistic regression model shows that GPA is a strong positive predictor of admission, meaning higher GPA increases the likelihood of being admitted. GRE scores also have a positive effect, though smaller. Undergraduate institution rank has a negative relationship with admission, indicating that students from lower-ranked institutions are less likely to be admitted.
